# FutureFunded Sister UI Review Checklist

Mode: test/demo only  
Live donations: not enabled yet  
Text-to-Donate: hidden until real SMS number is connected  

## Review links

- Platform homepage: https://getfuturefunded.com/platform/
- Campaign page: https://getfuturefunded.com/c/connect-atx-elite
- Launch workspace: https://getfuturefunded.com/platform/onboarding
- Operator login/dashboard: https://getfuturefunded.com/platform/dashboard
- Login: https://getfuturefunded.com/platform/login

## What to review

### Campaign page
- Does it feel trustworthy enough to send to families?
- Is the donation panel clear?
- Does the page feel too long or just right?
- Are the sponsor packages easy to understand?
- Does the story feel like Connect ATX Elite?
- Is anything confusing before someone gives?

### Platform homepage
- Does it explain FutureFunded clearly?
- Does it feel like a real product, not a side project?
- Is the demo campaign easy to find?
- Is the sponsor message clear?

### Onboarding
- Does the setup flow feel organized?
- Are the fields understandable?
- Does Text-to-Donate setup make sense as a future option?

### Login / Dashboard
- Does the operator area feel protected and professional?
- Does the access page feel trustworthy?

## Do not launch live until

- Stripe publishable key shows pk_live_
- Checkout creates cs_live_
- Webhook secret is live
- Receipt flow has been tested
- First real $1 donation test is intentionally completed
