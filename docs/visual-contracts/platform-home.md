# FutureFunded Platform Home Visual Contract

Route:

- `/platform/`

Template:

- `apps/web/app/templates/platform/index.html`

Purpose:

- Product homepage / platform landing page.
- Explains FutureFunded as the fundraising SaaS product.
- Shows sponsor-ready fundraising, campaign cockpit, sponsor engine, operations proof, flagship campaign proof, and final CTA.

Approved structure:

1. Product hero + campaign cockpit preview
2. Trust/readiness cards
3. Campaign type/audience section
4. How it works
5. Sponsor engine
6. Operations layer
7. Flagship campaign proof
8. Launch readiness proof
9. Final CTA

Do not replace this page with onboarding layout.

Separate surfaces:

- `/` = public homepage or redirect surface
- `/platform/` = platform product home
- `/platform/onboarding` = compact campaign setup flow
- `/c/connect-atx-elite` = public campaign page
- `/platform/dashboard` = operator dashboard
