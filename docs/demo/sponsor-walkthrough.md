# Sponsor Walkthrough

## What the sponsor should feel

- The campaign looks professional.
- Recognition is clear.
- Sponsorship is simple.
- The team will represent the business well.

## Sponsor path

1. Open the campaign page.
2. Scroll to sponsor packages.
3. Review Community, Featured, and Season tiers.
4. Select sponsor interest.
5. Submit or continue through sponsor flow.
6. Organizer reviews and follows up.

## Sponsor positioning

Use plain language:

> Your business can support the team and receive public recognition on a polished campaign page families will share.
