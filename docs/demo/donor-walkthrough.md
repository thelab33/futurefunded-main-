# Donor Walkthrough

## What the donor should feel

- This campaign is real.
- The team is credible.
- Giving is simple.
- Checkout is safe.
- Sharing is easy.

## Donor path

1. Open the campaign page.
2. Read the hero and progress.
3. Choose Give securely.
4. Pick an amount.
5. Continue to checkout.
6. Share the campaign after giving.

## Conversion hierarchy

Primary action:

- Give securely

Secondary actions:

- Sponsor the season
- Share campaign
