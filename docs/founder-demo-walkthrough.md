# FutureFunded Founder Demo Walkthrough

## Goal

Show FutureFunded as a sellable fundraising operating system, not just a campaign page.

## 7-minute flow

### 1. Open with the promise

FutureFunded helps youth teams, schools, clubs, and nonprofits launch sponsor-ready fundraising pages that feel trustworthy from day one.

### 2. Show the platform homepage

Point out:
- Premium public presentation
- Clear campaign demo path
- Sponsor package framing
- Operator-ready launch workflow

### 3. Open the campaign page

Show:
- emotional hero
- secure giving path
- sponsor packages
- share flow
- post-checkout trust copy

### 4. Explain the money loop

Say:
- A donor can give through Stripe Checkout.
- A sponsor can choose a package and complete checkout.
- The system verifies paid sessions.
- Ledger/idempotency protects against duplicates.
- Follow-up email is sent through Postmark.

### 5. Open onboarding

Frame it as the launch workspace:
- organization identity
- campaign story
- sponsor package
- payment readiness
- media and handoff

### 6. Open dashboard

Frame the locked dashboard as the protected operator console:
- campaign health
- donation ledger
- sponsor queue
- lifecycle follow-up

### 7. Close with the business case

FutureFunded can become a white-label fundraising SaaS for teams, schools, nonprofits, and clubs.

## Close line

This is no longer just a beautiful page. It is a working fundraising MVP with donation, sponsor, payment verification, lifecycle email, and operator workflow proof.
